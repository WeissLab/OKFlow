What you'll see

Judge the whole image. Some cards show the brightfield alone; judge those from the brightfield. Every cell comes back once, flipped or turned; just answer it again.

Cut off = toss, even when it's sharp. If any part of the cell runs off the edge of the image, whether a sliver or half of it, toss it. Dark blue areas are outside the camera's view: a cell that runs into one is cut off too.

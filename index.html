Instructions

New here, or want a recap?

Base your answer on the brightfield (the grey image on the left). When the fluorescence image is shown too, use it as an extra guide, for example to tell one cell from two, or a cell from a bead or debris. Fluorescence often looks soft even when the cell is sharp.

↑←→

On a computer, your arrow keys are shortcuts. → keep, ← reject, ↑ not sure. Return goes back a card and P pauses: you never need the mouse.

Some cards show the brightfield alone. Every cell comes back once, flipped or turned; just answer it again.

Cut off = reject, even when it's sharp. If any part of the cell runs off the edge of the image, whether a sliver or half of it, reject it. Dark blue areas are outside the camera's view: a cell that runs into one is cut off too.
